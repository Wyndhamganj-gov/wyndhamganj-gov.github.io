Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about using Apna WDM.

1. What is Apna WDM?

Apna WDM is a community app for Wyndhamganj and nearby villages, bringing together local news, chat, creators and ride booking.

2. Is Apna WDM free to use?

The app is free to use for news, chat and creator features. For a ride, the fare is paid directly to the rider.

3. How do I book a ride?

Open Book a Ride, allow location access when needed, enter pickup and destination, choose Personal or Sharing and select a vehicle type — Bike, E-Bike, Toto/E-Rickshaw or Car. Confirm the request so nearby approved riders can receive it.

4. What is the permanent 4-digit Ride OTP?

Your Ride screen displays a unique 4-digit code for ride verification. Share it only when the correct rider has arrived at your pickup point and you are boarding.

5. How do I pay for a ride?

Payment is made directly to the rider using cash or UPI/QR, as agreed with the rider. There is no platform commission according to the app's stated ride model.

6. How can I become a WDM Rider?

Open Become WDM Rider and submit your name, mobile number, email, vehicle type, vehicle number, address, profile details and requested vehicle photos. Rider applications are subject to admin approval.

7. What are Message Requests and Blocking?

A first-time message may appear in Message Requests. You can accept or ignore it. You can also block or unblock users through the app's available controls.

8. How can I get listed as a local creator?

Use the creator submission option inside the app and provide your public YouTube or Instagram profile details.

9. How can I contact support?

Email thehostwost@gmail.com or use the official @apna_wdm handle inside the app.
