Become a WDM Rider

Rider registration mein apna naam, mobile, email, vehicle type, vehicle number, address, profile aur vehicle photos submit karein. Application admin review/approval ke subject mein hai.

  • Local rider application
  • Vehicle information submission
  • Approval before rider listing
  • Fare/payment directly between passenger and rider

1. App and eligibility

Apna WDM is developed by Vivek Kumar (Independent Developer). By using the app, you agree to these terms. You must provide accurate registration information, keep your account secure and must not impersonate another person. Keep your permanent 4-digit Ride PIN/OTP private and share it only at the correct ride handover stage.

2. User content and conduct

You are responsible for content you post, upload or send. Illegal, defamatory, abusive, sexually explicit, hateful, misleading, threatening, violent or spam content is not permitted. Content may be removed and accounts may be restricted or suspended where appropriate.

3. Ride services

Apna WDM is a platform that helps connect passengers with local riders. It does not own vehicles or employ riders. Riders and passengers are responsible for complying with applicable Motor Vehicles Act requirements and other laws. Fare displays may be estimates; confirm the fare with the rider. Payments are made directly to the rider by cash or UPI. Apna WDM is not responsible for disputes between passengers and riders or for personal belongings. Verify the rider and vehicle before travelling; share the Ride OTP only after the correct rider/vehicle has arrived at pickup.

4. Availability and changes

The app is provided on an “as is” and “as available” basis. Continuous access, error-free operation or accurate GPS in areas with weak network coverage is not guaranteed. These terms may be updated from time to time.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2026

1. Information that may be collected

Account/profile details may include name, username, profile photo, date of birth, gender, village/locality, mobile number and email. The app may process posts, news submissions, photos and messages you choose to share. For an active ride booking, location may be used to support pickup and ride matching; the stated app design does not require background location outside ride use. Rider applications may include vehicle type/registration, photos, address and optional UPI ID.

2. How information is used

Information is used to operate profiles, the permanent 4-digit Ride OTP, feeds, chats, creators directory, notifications, rider review and passenger/rider connections, and to help prevent abuse.

3. Services and sharing

The app has been described as using Firebase Authentication/Cloud Firestore, Cloudinary and mapping/geocoding services including OpenStreetMap, Esri and Nominatim. Personal information is not intended to be sold, rented or traded to advertisers. Information may be processed by the service providers needed to run the app.

4. Your choices and account deletion

You can manage profile details and use block/unblock controls in the app where available. For account deletion requests, contact thehostwost@gmail.com. Requests may require account verification.

5. Children and policy updates

Apna WDM is not intended for children under 13. If information from a child under 13 is identified as having been collected without required parental consent, contact support to request review and deletion. This policy may be updated as the app changes.
